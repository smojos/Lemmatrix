## What counts as the artwork

- every image in `lemmatrix/resources/`: the program icon (`lemmatrix.svg`, `lemmatrix.ico` and
  the PNGs in `icons/`), the splash screen (`splash.svg`), the χμλ, Questions, Cogitate, To Do and
  Snapshots icons, and the ɛλicit drawings in `elicit/`;
- the ɛλicit web page's images in `docs/elicit/`: `favicon.svg`, `logo.svg`, `icon-180.png` and
  `icon-512.png`;
- the icons drawn in code: `lemmatrix/ui/icons.py` (every icon in the program, including the
  Texts and utterances bubbles) and `lemmatrix/ui/whiteboard_art.py` (the whiteboard's markers,
  duster and Blackboard icon);
- the designs themselves, in any form, size or colour: the λχ program icon, the λemmatriχ splash
  screen, the ɛλicit cloud and logo, and the other icons.

The code that loads and shows the artwork (for example `lemmatrix/ui/splash.py`,
`lemmatrix/elicit_art.py` and the scripts in `tools/`) is ordinary AGPL code.

## What you may do

- **Share Lemmatrix as it is.** Copies of Lemmatrix exactly as the author released them (from
  https://github.com/smojos/Lemmatrix or https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23512411) may be copied
  and shared freely, artwork included, unchanged.
- **Change Lemmatrix for your own use**, on your own computers, artwork included.

## What you may not do

- Use any of the artwork outside Lemmatrix, or change it and pass it on.
- Distribute a **modified version** of Lemmatrix, or another program, with any of the artwork in
  it. A modified version must remove all of it and replace every icon, the splash screen and the
  ɛλicit drawings with artwork of its own. Its program icon and splash screen must not look like
  Lemmatrix's.

To ask about any other use, write to lemmatrix.support@gmail.com.

## Letterforms

The splash screen's title is stored as outlines drawn from Doulos SIL (SIL Open Font License 1.1,
https://openfontlicense.org), and the ɛλ in the ɛλicit drawings from Arial letterforms; no font is
included. The Texts and utterances icon's letters are outlines from Charis SIL (also the SIL Open
Font License 1.1). The fonts keep their own licences.

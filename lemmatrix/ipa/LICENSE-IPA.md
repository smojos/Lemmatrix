# The IPA soundfiles

The soundfiles in this folder are **not part of Lemmatrix's own code or artwork**, and neither the
AGPL (LICENSE) nor ARTWORK.md covers them. They come from Wikimedia Commons' *General phonetics*
page (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/General_phonetics), **unchanged**, each under its own
licence:

- **CC BY-SA 3.0** (120 soundfiles): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
- **CC BY-SA 4.0** (4): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- **CC0** (1): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Public domain** (1)

`CREDITS.md` names the person who recorded each one, its licence and its Commons page, and
`credits.tsv` holds the same with the version used (its upload date and SHA-1). Lemmatrix shows
the credit when one is played (the hover on α or β in the comparison area's Segments tab).

They were downloaded with `tools/fetch_commons_soundfiles.py` on 24 October 2026, at the versions
pinned there. Many of these files have been edited back and forth on Commons, and several
description pages credit someone other than the person who recorded the current version; the
credits here were worked out from each file's upload history.

If you change one of the CC BY-SA soundfiles (trim it, change its level), your changed version must
be shared under the same licence (or a compatible later one), with the credit and a note that it
was changed.
